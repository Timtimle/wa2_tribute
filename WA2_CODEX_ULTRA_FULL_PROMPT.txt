FONT ROLE REQUIREMENTS:

- Japanese emotional/display text

7. HARD READABILITY REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

On a desktop viewport around 1920×1080:

- main English body copy: generally 18px–22px
- important secondary English text: generally at least 15px–17px
- navigation: generally at least 14px–16px
- character metadata / track information: comfortably readable
- tiny metadata below 12px should be extremely rare
- important information must NEVER be tiny

Do not use opacity to make text effectively unreadable.

Text contrast must remain comfortable against the dark background.

Do not compensate for poor hierarchy by making headlines absurdly large.

If uncertain between:
- a visually fashionable font
and
- a more readable game-oriented font

choose the readable game-oriented option.
