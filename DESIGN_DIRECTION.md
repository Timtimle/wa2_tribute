# WHITE ALBUM 2

A fan-made website dedicated to WHITE ALBUM 2, focused on winter, memory, music, silence, emotional distance, and the maturity of its story.

The site should feel cold, quiet, intimate, nostalgic, restrained, and slightly melancholic. Artwork from the visual novel should remain the main visual focus, while the interface stays subtle and supports the atmosphere rather than competing with it.

Snow, negative space, slow movement, soft lighting, and Japanese-inspired typography should shape the overall mood.

The experience should feel closer to a visual novel translated into a website than a conventional product page, portfolio, or modern technology landing page.

The visual direction should be cinematic and image-led, with large artwork, deliberate asymmetrical composition, text integrated into imagery, occasional vertical Japanese writing, and chapters presented like scenes or memories rather than ordinary website sections.

Typography should feel intentional, readable, elegant, and appropriate for a Japanese visual novel. Japanese display text may use Mincho-inspired typography, while English typography should be selected from high-quality game, anime, and visual-novel references rather than default editorial serif trends. English body text must remain comfortably readable and should never be reduced to tiny decorative copy.

The typography system should have clearly separated roles for title, chapter heading, character names, body text, navigation, music information, and small metadata. It should not rely on one decorative typeface for everything.

The design should be image-first. Character artwork, CGs, key visuals, cover art, and environmental imagery should carry most of the emotional weight, while interface elements remain secondary.

Character artwork should feel integrated into the page rather than placed inside generic cards or portrait boxes. Images may overlap layout regions, fade into backgrounds, use controlled masking, or appear as large silhouettes when appropriate.

Image placement should feel deliberate. Avoid random floating assets, repetitive rectangular frames, or gallery layouts that look like a component showcase. If the same artwork is reused, it should be presented through meaningfully different crops, scale, layering, or context.

Motion should remain slow and restrained. Snowfall, subtle parallax, image transitions, soft fades, masking, and atmospheric lighting should create depth without making the site feel flashy or expensive to render.

Snow should remain an important part of the atmosphere, but performance takes priority over particle count. The site should aim to feel smooth and close to 60 FPS on a normal desktop GPU.

The background should remain dark and wintery, but clean. Avoid rough grain, obvious noise textures, heavy blur, or effects that make the page look dirty or reduce performance.

Music should feel like part of the visual-novel experience rather than a separate web player. The Music Room should resemble a polished game or VN sound-mode screen, with restrained controls, clear track hierarchy, and artwork integrated into the presentation.

The English writing should be understated and natural. Avoid marketing language, design commentary, fake-poetic filler, and sentences that sound like an AI describing its own composition. Shorter and more emotionally restrained writing is preferred.

High-quality visual references from visual novels, Japanese games, anime promotional sites, and premium character pages may be studied for typography hierarchy, character composition, layering, image integration, navigation restraint, transitions, and polish.

Reference sites should never be copied literally. Each reference should be used only for the specific design problem it solves, while WHITE ALBUM 2 remains the primary art direction.

Avoid generic AI website patterns such as oversized fashionable serif headings, tiny uppercase metadata, excessive letter spacing, repeated card-based layouts, decorative boxes, glassmorphism, blue-purple startup gradients, meaningless graphic elements, and unnecessary grain or noise textures.

The final result should feel mature, emotional, wintery, polished, readable, image-driven, and unmistakably connected to WHITE ALBUM 2.
